# 家庭理智大作戰 · Family Sanity Showdown

A family card battle with pack opening and deck building, starring **Jeff** (Dad), **Erin** (Mom), **Josh** (10, big brother) and **Jasper** (8, little brother).
Bring your opponent's **Sanity Points (SP)** down to 0. Play vs the computer (easy / normal) or 2 players on one device (hot-seat with a pass screen). Chinese and English.

**Play:** https://ywchen0711.github.io/family-sanity-showdown/

## Collect, open packs, build decks
- **500 cards** in four rarities: common, rare, epic, legendary (33 hand-picked legendaries).
- **Packs** (5 cards): 3 mostly-common slots, 1 rare-or-better slot, 1 wild slot. A legendary is guaranteed within 10 packs.
  The Family Pack costs 🪙100; the four hero packs (🪙120) roll half their cards from that hero's signature cards.
- **Coins**: win vs the normal computer 60 (loss 20), easy 35 (loss 10), a 2-player game 25. One free pack every day.
  Copies beyond what a deck can use turn into coins; coins can also **craft** a specific card (40 / 100 / 250 / 600).
- **Decks**: 30 cards, at most 2 copies of a card and 1 of a legendary, up to 8 decks. New players start with every common
  card from the base set, a ready-made deck for each family member, and 3 free packs. The editor has auto-fill and a cost curve.
- Progress is saved in the browser and synced with the lobby's cloud save / account.

## How a turn works
1. **Start of turn**: max energy +1 (up to 10), energy refills. Location, poison/regen, visiting characters and items trigger.
2. **Draw phase**: draw 1 card (+ location bonus, + Josh's passive). When the deck runs out, the discard pile is shuffled
   into a new deck for 6 SP (12 the next time, …).
3. **Main phase**: play any cards you can pay for.
4. **End turn**: discard down to 8 cards. At 0 SP you lose; after 60 turns the higher SP wins.

## The database
`data/cards.json` (also bundled as `js/cards.data.js`) is generated by `tools/build-cards.mjs` from the base set (180 cards)
and `tools/cards-expansion.mjs` (320 cards):

| Type | Count | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| character | 60 | 4 heroes + 56 visitors who help for a few turns (max 2 at once) |
| action | 200 | instant effects: damage, heal, draw, energy, steal, discard, shield, poison, max energy, refill, cleanse… |
| item | 140 | stay for a few turns (max 3): per-turn effects, damage boost, armor, thorns |
| location | 100 | one at a time; changes rules for everyone + a bonus for its owner |

Every card has `id, name{zh,en}, type, cost, effect{zh,en}, value, rarity, set`, plus machine-readable effects
(`fx`, `tick`, `mods`, `rule`, `owner`, `turns`) used by the engine. Rarity is ranked by power for cost within each type;
legendaries are chosen by hand.

## Code
- `js/engine.js` — pure rules engine (no DOM): `newGame` (with your own decks), `startTurn`, `playCard`, `endTurn`, `costOf`, `cpuPlan`, `cpuTurn`.
- `js/collection.js` — pure collection logic: profiles, pack odds and pity, crafting, coins, deck rules, auto-build.
- `js/ui.js`, `js/i18n.js`, `style.css` — menus, shop and pack opening, collection, deck editor, the board, the computer player.
- `npm test` — rebuilds the database and checks counts and rarities, the rules, pack odds over 2000 packs, deck rules,
  and hero balance across 800 simulated computer games.
