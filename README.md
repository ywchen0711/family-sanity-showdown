# 家庭理智大作戰 · Family Sanity Showdown

A simple, fast card battle for the family: **Jeff** (Dad), **Erin** (Mom), **Josh** (10, big brother) and **Jasper** (8, little brother).
Bring your opponent's **Sanity Points (SP)** down to 0. Play vs the computer (easy / normal) or 2 players on one device (hot-seat with a pass screen). Chinese and English.

**Play:** https://ywchen0711.github.io/family-sanity-showdown/

## How a turn works
1. **Start of turn**: max energy +1 (up to 10), energy refills. Location, poison/regen, visiting characters and items trigger.
2. **Draw phase**: draw 1 card (+ location bonus, + Josh's passive). An empty deck causes escalating fatigue damage.
3. **Main phase**: play any cards you can pay for.
4. **End turn**: discard down to 8 cards. If either side is at 0 SP the game ends; after 60 turns the higher SP wins.

## The family
| Hero | SP | Start hand | Passive |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jeff 👨 | 24 | 4 | **Dad Joke**: your damaging cards deal +1 |
| Erin 👩 | 26 | 4 | **Super Mom**: healing +2, items cost 1 less |
| Josh 👦 | 30 | 5 | **Quick Thinker**: draw +1 in the draw phase with 3 or fewer cards in hand |
| Jasper 🧒 | 20 | 4 | **Cute Face**: the first 3+ hit from a card each opponent turn is reduced by 1; **Meltdown**: below 8 SP his cards deal +1 |

Cards marked ★ are a hero's signature cards (+1 when that hero plays them).

## The database: 180 cards
`data/cards.json` (also bundled as `js/cards.data.js`) is generated by `tools/build-cards.mjs`:

| Type | Count | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| character | 20 | 4 heroes + 16 visitors who help for a few turns (max 2 at once) |
| action | 70 | instant effects: damage, heal, draw, energy, steal, discard, shield, poison… |
| item | 50 | stay for a few turns (max 3): per-turn effects, damage boost, armor, thorns |
| location | 40 | one at a time; changes rules for everyone + a bonus for its owner |

Every card has `id, name{zh,en}, type, cost, effect{zh,en}, value`, plus machine-readable effects (`fx`, `tick`, `mods`, `rule`, `owner`, `turns`) used by the engine.

## Code
- `js/engine.js` — pure rules engine (no DOM): `newGame`, `startTurn`, `playCard`, `endTurn`, `costOf`, `canPlay`, `cpuPlan`, `cpuTurn`. The random seed lives in the state so games can be cloned for the AI's look-ahead.
- `js/ui.js`, `js/i18n.js`, `style.css` — menus, the board, the card library, the CPU player.
- `npm test` — rebuilds the database and checks the counts, the rules, and the balance across 800 simulated CPU games.
